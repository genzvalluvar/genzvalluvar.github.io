Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GenZ Valluvar (“GenZ Kural”, “we”, “us”) handles information on this website (genzvalluvar.github.io) and in our private content publishing tool, ARIVU.

1. Who we are

GenZ Valluvar is an independent Tamil content creator that publishes original short couplets (“kurals”) and their meanings on social media platforms including Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube and Pinterest.

2. What ARIVU is

ARIVU is an internal tool used only by the GenZ Valluvar team to schedule and publish our own original content (images, videos, titles and descriptions) to our own social media accounts, and to read basic performance statistics (such as impressions, saves, likes and followers) for our own accounts. ARIVU is not offered to the public and does not let other people log in.

3. Information we collect

  • Website visitors: this website does not use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking, and does not ask you for any personal information. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type for security purposes, under GitHub’s own privacy policy.
  • Platform data (via APIs): when ARIVU connects to a platform such as Pinterest, we access only our own account and use only the permissions needed to create Pins, manage our own boards and read our own account and Pin analytics.
  • We do not collect personal data about other Pinterest users or other people’s accounts, and we do not read private messages.

4. How we use information

  • To publish our own content to our own accounts.
  • To view performance statistics for our own content so we can improve it.

5. Access tokens and security

Platform access tokens are stored securely as encrypted secrets (for example, GitHub Actions encrypted secrets). They are never published in code, shared with third parties or used for any purpose other than the uses described above.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data from platform APIs is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep only basic statistics about our own posts, for as long as they are useful to us. You can ask us any question about your data, or request deletion of anything related to you, by emailing us. We can disconnect ARIVU from any platform at any time, which revokes its access.

8. Children

This website and our tool are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Third-party platforms

When you visit our pages on Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube or Pinterest, those platforms’ own privacy policies apply.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
